Nijmegen, Netherlands — April 17, 2026 — ScreenPoint Medical, a leader in artificial intelligence solutions for breast cancer detection and risk assessment, today announced $14 million in new funding from existing investors, global software investor Insight Partners and Siemens Healthineers, in addition to $2 million in non-dilutive research grants.
